# 📡 Broadcast Without Borders — Project Aurora Sender

> *“A signal is only as alive as the hands that keep it moving.”*

Welcome to **Project Aurora Sender**, an open-source, ultra-lightweight live video transmission framework that began life as a quiet experiment in resilient streaming and has since grown into a full broadcasting companion for creators, educators, community organizers, and anyone who refuses to let distance mute a moment.

Where the original **live-sender** project explored the foundations of a Live Video Experience (LiVE) pipeline, **Aurora Sender** takes that spark and turns it into a layered, extensible, and remarkably friendly toolkit. It is a sunrise for your stream — a system that wakes up fast, adapts to rough networks, and keeps the picture flowing even when the path is narrow.

This is not just another encoder wrapper. It is a philosophy: *every voice deserves a clear channel.*

---

## 🌅 What Is Aurora Sender?

Aurora Sender is a **modular live video transmission suite**. It captures, transcodes, packages, and delivers media with an emphasis on:

- **Stability over sheer speed** — adaptive pacing that respects the network you actually have.
- **Clarity over complexity** — a readable, hackable codebase that welcomes newcomers.
- **Longevity over trends** — predictable interfaces, well-documented internals, and zero lock-in.

In 2026, streaming is everywhere, but *dependable* streaming is still rare. Aurora Sender exists to close that gap.

## 🚀 Getting Started (No Terminal Incantations Required)

Aurora Sender is designed to be approachable. You do not need to memorize mystical command sequences. Instead:

1. **Download the release bundle** for your platform.
2. **Unpack it** wherever you keep your tools.
3. **Launch the Aurora Sender companion** from your applications menu.
4. **Follow the first-run wizard**, which walks you through capture, quality, and destination choices.
5. **Press Start.** Watch the sunrise.

If you prefer configuring things by hand, every setting is exposed in a plainly written configuration file with comments. No hidden magic.

## 🧭 Architecture at a Glance

Aurora Sender is organized into cooperative layers:

- **Ingest Layer** — pulls frames from cameras, screens, or files.
- **Processing Layer** — applies scaling, filtering, and composition.
- **Encoding Layer** — compresses with configurable quality targets.
- **Transport Layer** — moves encoded data across the network.
- **Control Layer** — orchestrates everything and exposes telemetry.

Each layer speaks a narrow, documented contract. Swap one, keep the rest. This is the secret to the project's longevity.
